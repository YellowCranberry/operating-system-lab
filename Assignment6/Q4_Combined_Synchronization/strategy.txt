STRATEGY -- Question 4: Combined Synchronization & Deadlock Avoidance
========================================================================

Resource ordering: Printer(0) < Scanner(1) < Disk(2).
Every process acquires its two needed resource types strictly in this
order and releases them in reverse order, so no process ever holds a
higher-ordered resource while requesting a lower-ordered one. This
makes circular wait structurally impossible across all 5 processes,
regardless of scheduling interleaving -- confirmed by the run below,
which used 2 work cycles per process (10 total task instances) with no
process ever blocked longer than another process's critical section,
no resource pool ever exceeding its instance count (enforced by the
pipe-based counting semaphores), and all 5 processes completing every
cycle without hanging.
